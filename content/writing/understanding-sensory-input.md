# On Sensory Input & Neural Translation

Most of my time is spent trying to understand how the brain handles sensory input and how we can translate neural activity into something useful.

## The Gap Between Intent & Input

Human interaction with digital systems has historically relied on physical proxies: keyboards, mice, touchscreens, and trackpads. However, these physical proxies introduce friction between cognitive intent and system execution.

### Neural Encoding

Sensory perception operates through continuous parallel streams of neural spikes across cortical populations. Translating these high-dimensional neural signals requires:

1. **Dimensionality Reduction**: Mapping complex population dynamics into intent vectors.
2. **Real-time Adaptation**: Accounting for non-stationary neural drift over extended interaction sessions.

## Closing the Loop

By designing interfaces that adapt dynamically to biological feedback, we can reduce cognitive load and move closer to seamless human-machine symbiosis.
