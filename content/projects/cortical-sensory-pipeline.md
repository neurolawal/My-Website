# Cortical Sensory Pipeline

A modular preprocessing and feature extraction pipeline built for analyzing high-density cortical electrophysiology recordings.

## Pipeline Architecture

1. **Raw Signal Filtering**: Bandpass and notch filtering to eliminate 50/60 Hz powerline interference.
2. **Spike Sorting & Alignment**: Automated waveform detection and principal component clustering.
3. **Spectral Feature Extraction**: Local field potential (LFP) power spectral density estimation.

## Purpose

Designed to accelerate neural data preprocessing, enabling rapid hypothesis testing during sensory stimulation protocols.
