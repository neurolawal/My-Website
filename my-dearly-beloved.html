For Keziah / One Month

My Dearly Beloved

✦

I will like to tell you a story Keziah, of a boy, and bear with me, as I can promise a sort of happy ending. I recount the tale of our meeting enough times to make uncomfortable, I know, and it comes to mind again tonight as I recall the green-behind-the-ears, fresh out-of-trouble, relatively thoughtless 17 year old, that left his house at the other end of Kwara State that day. I also can recall that he knew he would rather be anywhere but where he was at the time, he knew that he was certainly scared of the the place ahead, he just did not know that he will meet a miracle.

I genuinely cannot recall what I said or what led to what, hell, that whole day always plays out as three flashes in my moments of recollection. There’s my consciousness of how rumpled my short sleeved brown native clothes were. Then me seeing a dashing young lady (wink, wink) just after I scaled the last step of the stairs. And finally some apparition of Idris mouthing some things I definitely cannot recall. All these, remain motifs to your “I don’t knowwww”; the highlight of that day, the day I met a miracle. A boy met a miracle, and began to hope on his becoming a man, one worthy of such wonder.

In the words of Apostle Paul, my dearly beloved, I would have you know that I do not take everything this is, as anything other than the privilege that it is. This is me knowing you early enough to grow up together and with all my hope, well enough to grow old together, this is my dream :— manifest.

I find myself living out an ideal christian life of love through you (which I must confess, was one experience that solidified the logic of my emotions for you) and more of that, I want to do. To seek your becoming. To have you happy. To keep you in my thoughts. To be sensitive to your needs. To believe your entirety. I find you becoming the perfect neighbor for me.

Even when I try to quieten my heart and look at us rationally, I find you worth everything I have on the table, everything I have to offer; and then some. I’ll use this to quickly acknowledge my initial concerns about the possibility of a miss, from the kindlings of my love for you which the years past were, to these past few months of emotions becoming a decision, they were all spotted with those concerns. However, there always was a light of possibility present, the possibility of an existence where I am a peaceful part of your life, a possibility that pales all concerns. That is what this feels like, and what I pray you ever are to me, an overwhelming pull in the right direction, a pull I’ll always oblige.

I am utterly besotted by your being and will continue to be (I like it here :)). This feeling is one I want to continually stoke and constantly communicate. I will always let you in on the contents of my yearning, my undying want of you. One notion I came across that aptly captures my person as It concerns you is how I selfishly want to be the only one who can see you the way I do.

To wrap this up,

In the “then”, you were a desire.

In the “then” that came after that, you became my muse.

In the “now”, you are my beloved.

In the “thens” that will come after this, you will continue to be and have my devotion.

I believe I am I your debt, I owe you the world, and this is a debt I want to begin to, then continue to pay for as long as you will have me. With all my heart, I love you.

I wish I could have done the asking out in person, please take this as a token of my yearning for you.

Here is to one month, and one more, always.

With all my heart & devotion, Samuel
